# Community

How to join OpenCritique Commons discussion and what to expect from maintainers.

## How to join

1. Read [START_HERE.md](../START_HERE.md) and pick **Track A** (adapters) or
   **Track B** (scientist / lab pilots).
2. Skim [CONTRIBUTING_TIERS.md](CONTRIBUTING_TIERS.md) for the reading load that
   matches your change.
3. Open a labeled issue or PR. Prefer
   [good first issues](https://github.com/fraware/OpenCritique-Commons/issues?q=is%3Aissue+is%3Aopen+label%3A%22good+first+issue%22)
   when you want a bounded first contribution.
4. Follow the [Code of Conduct](../CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

Questions about process, adapters, or private pilots are welcome via GitHub
Issues (use the Docs, Adapter, Pilot, or Engineering templates). Prefer Issues
(or Discussions when enabled) over private channels for non-security topics so
others can learn from the thread.

## Discussion norms

- Be precise about **evidence class**: sample vs private_live vs production.
- Do not frame sample fixtures or private `runs/` as scientific performance
  validation or as a public reviewer leaderboard.
- Keep confidential manuscript text and credentials out of public issues and
  fixtures.
- Disclose role overlap when the same people occupy conflicting evaluation roles
  (alpha staffing is thin; honesty beats appearance of independence).
- Assume good faith; ask for clarification before attributing bad intent.

## Office hours

Maintainers aim for **biweekly** office hours (async thread or live call). Times
and links are announced in a pinned issue or discussion when scheduled. Between
sessions, use issues; acknowledgment target is documented in
[MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md).

## Response expectations

| Item | Target |
|---|---|
| Issue / PR acknowledgment | Within **72 hours** on business days (see MAINTAINERS) |
| Security reports | [SECURITY.md](../SECURITY.md) — not public issues |
| Claim-boundary questions | Treated as high priority; unauthorized claims stay locked |

## Adopters and registry

Organizations and tools using the schemas or adapters can be listed via PR in
[ADOPTERS.md](../ADOPTERS.md). Community adapter listings (when present) live in
[community-adapters.md](community-adapters.md). Listing is recognition of
interoperability intent, **not** endorsement of reviewer quality.

## What this community is not

- A venue for unlocked precision/recall marketing
- A place to fabricate production authenticity evidence
- A hosted production ops product (see deferred specs linked from
  [MILESTONES.md](MILESTONES.md))
