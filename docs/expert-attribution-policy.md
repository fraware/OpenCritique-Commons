# Expert attribution opt-in policy (issue #14)

**Policy version:** `expert-attribution-v0.1`

## Default

Public attribution is **opt-in** and independent from compensation. The default
is anonymous / pseudonymous credit in public materials.

## Opt-in

Experts may consent in writing (or via a recorded registry preference) to:

- name or preferred credit string on acknowledgments pages,
- optional link (personal site / ORCID),
- domain-profile listing without task-level decision disclosure.

Consent must identify scope (which publications / releases) and may be revoked
prospectively. Revocation does not erase already published editions; subsequent
releases drop the credit.

## Opt-out / anonymity

- Task payloads and blinded studio views must not require real-world identity.
- Internal compensation records are not public attribution.
- Conflict disclosures remain mandatory even when attribution is declined.

## Prohibited

- Conditioning payment on public name credit.
- Publishing individual adjudication decisions as personal performance scores.
- Using attribution lists to imply endorsement of a evaluated system's quality.

## Claim boundary

Attribution policy is operational only. It does not unlock scientific
performance claims.
