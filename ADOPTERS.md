# Adopters

Organizations, labs, and tools that use OpenCritique Commons **schemas,
adapters, or evaluation interchange**. Listing here means interoperability
interest or usage — **not** endorsement of reviewer quality, and **not**
authorization of scientific performance claims.

`performance_claims_authorized` remains **false** until §12 / v0.9 gates are
met with real evidence. See [docs/MILESTONES.md](docs/MILESTONES.md).

## How to add your name

1. Confirm you can describe an honest **evidence class**:
   `sample` | `private_live` | `production` (production only with real rights
   and authenticity docs — never invent MANIFEST readiness).
2. Open a PR that adds a row to the table below.
3. Optionally also list an adapter in
   [docs/community-adapters.md](docs/community-adapters.md) /
   [docs/community-adapters.json](docs/community-adapters.json).
4. Keep claims language locked; do not market rankings or precision/recall as
   authorized results.
